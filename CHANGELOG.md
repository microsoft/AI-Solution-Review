## 20261016.1

- **File:** `AgentReviewCenter_20261016.1_managed.zip` (1.6 MB)
- **SHA-256:** `DBBF03DCCC2F4F6E208D5BFF70C2260A13E11AC2F74B6CE2AE07DD9673BB364B`

First public release of the Agent Review Center as a standalone managed solution.

- **Review three kinds of asset** — Copilot Studio agents in your environment, and declarative agents
  and skills uploaded as files.
- **Scored results** — a grade and rolled-up score across every agent in a review, with per-agent
  scores and a risk-ranked findings list that cites its sources.
- **Architecture map** — the components of an agent and the relationships between them, as a map or
  an accessible list.
- **Runtime analysis** — errors, latency, and knowledge usage from your agents' conversation
  transcripts, over a date range you choose.
- **Agent Debugger** — one conversation step by step, with execution flow, agent insights, a
  performance timeline, and estimated Copilot Credits.
- **Snapshots and progress** — review again to create a new snapshot and track the score over time.
- **Reports** — download results as PDF or JSON.
- **Setup** — a one-time setup app and an in-app readiness checklist.

Requires the [prerequisites](guides/prerequisites.md). Install with the
[install guide](guides/install.md).
